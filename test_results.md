## [IN] What are the loan categories under Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana?
**PASS** | score 0.744 | 36.8s | abstained=False facts_found=True scheme_in_sources=True

The loan categories under Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana are 'Shishu', 'Kishore', 'Tarun', and 'Tarun Plus'. 

- 'Shishu' covers loans up to Rs. 50,000.  
- 'Kishore' covers loans above Rs. 50,000 and up to Rs. 5 lakhs.  
- 'Tarun' covers loans above Rs. 5 lakhs and up to Rs. 10 lakhs.  
- 'Tarun Plus' covers loans up to Rs. 20 lakhs for entrepreneurs who have successfully repaid previous loans under the 'Tarun' category.

The subsidy for rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana varies based on the household's average monthly electricity consumption. The subsidy support is as follows:

- For households with average monthly consumption of 0–150 units: ₹30,000 to ₹60,000  
- For households with average monthly consumption of 150–300 units: ₹60,000 to ₹78,000  
- For households with average monthly consumption above 300 units: ₹78,000  

This information is provided in the benefits section of the scheme [2], and the subsidy covers up to 40% of the cost of solar panels as part of the overall scheme [1].
